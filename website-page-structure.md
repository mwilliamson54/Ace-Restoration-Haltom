# Website Page Structure: Restoration Services, Haltom City TX & Surrounding Areas

Complete page inventory for Ace Restoration's Fort Worth-first strategy. `{Brand}` = Ace Restoration. Homepage primary target: water damage restoration Fort Worth, TX.

## 1. Google best-practice rules applied

- **URLs:** lowercase, hyphen-separated, short, descriptive, max 3 levels deep, no parameters, no trailing-slash inconsistency (always use trailing slash).
- **Hierarchy:** Home > Service hub > Sub-service; Home > Service Areas > City > City x Service. Every page reachable within 3 clicks from `/`.
- **Breadcrumbs:** visible on every page except Home, marked up with `BreadcrumbList` schema.
- **Titles/H1:** one unique `<title>` (about 50-60 chars) and one H1 per page; unique meta description (about 150 chars).
- **Unique content:** no doorway pages. Each City x Service page needs 400-600+ words of genuinely local content (housing age, local causes, response process, FAQs, local proof). Neighborhoods are sections on city pages, not separate URLs.
- **Schema:** `LocalBusiness` (or `HomeAndConstructionBusiness`) on Home/Contact/city pages with `areaServed`; `Service` on service pages; `FAQPage` where real FAQs exist; `BreadcrumbList` everywhere; `Review`/`AggregateRating` only for genuine reviews.
- **NAP consistency:** same Name, Address, Phone everywhere and matching your Google Business Profile. If you serve areas without a physical address there, don't present fake addresses.
- **Technical:** HTTPS, mobile-first responsive, Core Web Vitals passing, self-referencing canonicals, `sitemap.xml` submitted in Search Console, `robots.txt`, custom 404, 301 redirects for any retired URL.
- **Internal linking:** hub pages link down to children; children link up to parent and across to siblings; each City x Service page links to its city page, its service hub, and 2-3 neighboring cities. Use descriptive anchor text.
- **Noindex:** thank-you page, HTML sitemap is optional (index or noindex), internal search/utility URLs.
