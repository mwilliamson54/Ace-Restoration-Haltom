What restoration services are available?

Ace Restoration's main service categories are water damage restoration, fire and smoke damage restoration, and mold removal and remediation. Water services can include emergency water removal, flood damage cleanup, extraction, structural drying, dehumidification, and response to burst pipes or appliance leaks. Fire restoration can involve soot and smoke cleanup, odor management, contents cleaning, and coordination of repairs. Mold work must be carried out within applicable Texas licensing rules and may require separately qualified assessment and remediation professionals.

Some projects also involve sewage cleanup, storm-related water intrusion, or reconstruction. These are not interchangeable tasks.
